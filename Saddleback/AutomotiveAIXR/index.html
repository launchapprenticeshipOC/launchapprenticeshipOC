Automotive Course Mapper

Skill groups stay with their occupation. Swap the courses they use, assign courses to individual competencies, choose which occupation counts each course's hours, and watch program hours update on the spot.

Notes and loading a new export

Starting data is the Saddleback CourseMapping sheet in Smartsheet, read on November 16, 2026, with Master Technician and Helper rows already realigned. Course units come from the Saddleback General Automotive Technician A.S. catalog page. AUTO 208 is not in that page, so its 3 units come from your module pivot. AUTO 104A and 104B are separate 5-unit, 90-hour courses.

Skill groups cannot be moved between occupations. They stay where the sheet puts them. What moves is the course: each course counts toward exactly one occupation, chosen in the course ledger, and its hours count once. Courses that are unassigned are not counted. Because each course counts once, the hours by occupation always add up to the program total.

A course is bound to a skill group when its competencies point to it, and that is separate from which occupation counts its hours. Removing a course from the last group that uses it also unassigns it. Replacing a course assigns the new one to the group's occupation if it was unassigned. Assigning every course to one occupation is the migration.

The Competencies tab sets the course for each competency on its own. A change there overrides the group-level course for that competency only, and a later group-level replace or remove still applies to it. The sheet CSV carries these per-competency courses.

The Live view puts the mapper and the Sankey side by side and animates every change. The last Sankey column shows which occupation counts each course. A competency taught by two cross-listed courses counts half toward each in the flow. A course counted without any competencies, such as AUTO 207, shows as a thin band.

The sheet CSV keeps the sheet's five columns. The course list CSV has the columns of the CID form: course code, title, occupation, and total course hours.

To load a fresh export, use File, Export, Export sheet to CSV in Smartsheet. The sheet needs the columns Occupation, Occupational Skills, Competency #, Competency, and Course.
