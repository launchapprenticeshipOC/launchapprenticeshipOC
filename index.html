Tool 1

Saddleback Course Mapper

Maps Saddleback's automotive courses to occupations and competencies, and shows how program hours change when you swap courses or change which occupation counts a course.

Saddleback/AutomotiveAIXR/
What is inside
  • Live view: skill groups beside an animated Sankey chart that redraws on every change.
  • Course mapper: the same editing with a full panel of totals and a course ledger.
  • Sankey flow: the chart at full size, with a competency list for the selected node.
  • Competencies: set the course for each competency, or tick several and apply a course to all.
  • Counting rules: skill groups stay with their occupation. A course counts once, under the occupation you assign it to. Hours are units times 18. Unassigned courses are not counted.
  • Exports: a sheet CSV in the sheet's five columns, and a course list CSV with the CID form's fields.

Tool 2

CHW and Allied Health Pathways

Matches Orange County college programs to the Community Health Worker standard and related allied health and behavioral health standards.

CHWPathways/
What is inside
  • Overview: a grid of occupations by colleges, with program counts and dots for the types present.
  • By occupation: each standard's DAS number, O*NET and RAPIDS codes, status, and LEA colleges, with programs by college.
  • By college: everything one college offers, grouped by LAUNCH occupation.
  • Template mode: load a Smartsheet CSV for another pathway, then set the title and focus occupation.
Open the tool
Tool 3

DAS Approved Standards

Browses the LAUNCH apprenticeship standards by program, occupation, and LEA college.

DASapprovedStandards/
What is inside
  • Overview: one tile per program, grouped by sector, with its DAS file number and a status bar.
  • By program: each occupation with O*NET and RAPIDS codes, status, progress, and LEA colleges.
  • By LEA college: every occupation a college is an LEA for.
  • Search and filter: by occupation, code, DAS number, or college, and by status.
Open the tool
Tool 4

Production Technician Pathway Planner

Plans course pathways for the Production Technician apprenticeship at Saddleback, checked against the standards' competencies.

ProductionTech/Saddleback_Starfish/
What is inside
  • Built on the program standards, DAS file 99764, LAUNCH Industrial Technology & Manufacturing.
  • Shows alternate course pathways for an incumbent-worker plan across Advanced Manufacturing, Graphic Communication, and Electronic Technology.
  • A pathway is valid as long as it meets the standards' competency checklist. The sample related instruction courses are a guide only.
Open the tool

Using the tools

Online

Open any tool from this page. They are published with GitHub Pages, so a link is all you need to share one.

Locally

Clone the repo and open a tool's index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.

Saved edits

Edits in the Course Mapper are saved in your own browser. They stay on your machine and are not sent anywhere.

Headings and body text use Barlow from Google Fonts. Offline, pages fall back to Arial Narrow and system fonts. Pages follow your light or dark setting.

Design conventions

All tools follow the LAUNCH design board. Orange and sky are used as fills, not as text on white. Headlines use Barlow Condensed Bold and body text uses Barlow. The wordmark gradient appears only as a thin band.

Notes

Known limits

  • Sample data is a point-in-time snapshot and does not update on its own.
  • AUTO 208 appears in the module pivot and the sheet but not on the Saddleback catalog page used for course units. Its 3 units come from the pivot.
  • LEA names and sector groupings come from the sheets as read.

Published data

Everything embedded in these pages is visible to anyone with the link. Keep Smartsheet links, contact details, and names of individual employees or apprentices out of the files.
